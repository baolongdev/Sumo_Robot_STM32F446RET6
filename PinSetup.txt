MCU: STM32F446RET6

---------------------------
STATUS & INPUT
- LED_STATUS: PB2        (OUTPUT)
- BTN_B0: PB0            (INPUT)
- BTN_C13: PC13          (INPUT)
- SPEAKER_TX: PB10
- SPEAKER_RX: PC5

---------------------------
LINE SENSOR (I2C)
- LINE_SDA: PB7          (AF I2C - BIDIR, YELLOW)
- LINE_SCL: PB6          (AF I2C - BIDIR, WHITE)

---------------------------
ULTRASONIC SENSORS

LEFT
- US_LEFT_TRIG: PA7      (OUTPUT)
- US_LEFT_ECHO: PC4      (INPUT)

MID
- US_MID_TRIG: PA5       (OUTPUT)
- US_MID_ECHO: PA6       (INPUT)

RIGHT
- US_RIGHT_TRIG: PA3     (OUTPUT)
- US_RIGHT_ECHO: PA4     (INPUT)

---------------------------
MOTOR DRIVER

LEFT MOTOR
- MOTOR_LEFT_R_EN: PC1   (OUTPUT)
- MOTOR_LEFT_L_EN: PC3   (OUTPUT)
- MOTOR_LEFT_PWM: PA1    (AF PWM)

RIGHT MOTOR
- MOTOR_RIGHT_R_EN: PC2  (OUTPUT)
- MOTOR_RIGHT_L_EN: PA0  (OUTPUT)
- MOTOR_RIGHT_PWM: PA2   (AF PWM)
